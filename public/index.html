Wiring Table: ESP32 to Buzzer & Magnetic Lock LED

No Servo SG90 or LCD required! Real retail security gates use Electromagnetic Solenoid Locks. The ESP32's onboard LED on GPIO 2 indicates when the lock is released (Gate Open), paired with audio buzzer chimes on GPIO 14.

🎵 Buzzer Audio Feedback Codes:

  • 1 Short Chirp (1800 Hz, 100ms): Barcode scanned & item added to bill.
  • 2 Mid Beeps (1200 Hz): Entry Gate Opened (Customer validated).
  • 3 High Victory Chimes (523Hz - 1046Hz): PayPal Auto-Payment Completed & Exit Gate Opened!
  • 1 Long Low Buzz (500 Hz, 600ms): Invalid QR / Unrecognized Barcode.

How the ESP32 communicates:

  1. ESP32 connects to your Wi-Fi network.
  2. Polls the server endpoint GET /api/esp32/poll every 400ms.
  3. When a command arrives (e.g. OPEN_ENTRY_GATE or BEEP_SCAN), the ESP32 plays the buzzer tone and turns the GPIO 2 LED ON.
  4. After 4 seconds, the gate lock automatically resets to Locked (LED OFF).
